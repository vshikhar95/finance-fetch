finance-fetch

finance-fetch is a personal, single-user OAuth client. A script on its owner's computer uses it to find bank, card and investment statement emails in his own Gmail accounts and download their attachments to that computer, for his personal bookkeeping.

It is not a public service. No one else uses it, and it has no sign-up.

It requests only the read-only Gmail scope (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change or delete mail.
