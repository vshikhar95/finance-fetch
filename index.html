finance-fetch

finance-fetch is a personal OAuth client for one household. A script on its owner's computer uses it to find bank, card and investment statement emails in the Gmail accounts of the owner and his immediate family (each of whom signs in and consents themselves), and downloads their attachments to that computer for the household's own bookkeeping.

It is not a public service. Only the owner's household uses it, and it has no sign-up.

It requests only the read-only Gmail scope (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change or delete mail.
