Privacy policy

finance-fetch is used only by its owner and members of his immediate family, each with their own Google account. Each person signs in and grants access themselves, and can revoke it at any time. This policy covers that use.

  • Data accessed: read-only access to the Gmail account of each household member who signed in (gmail.readonly). The script searches for statement emails from banks and financial institutions and saves their attachments on the owner's computer.
  • Data collected: none. There are no servers, analytics, cookies, or third parties. The app runs entirely on the owner's own computer, and message content is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Sharing: nothing is shared, sold, or transferred to anyone outside the household.
  • Retention and deletion: the downloaded files stay on the owner's computer and are deleted on request. Each member can revoke this app's access to their account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, at any time.
  • Google API data: use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
