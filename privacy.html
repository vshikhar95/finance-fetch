Privacy policy

finance-fetch is used by exactly one person: its owner, with his own Google accounts. This policy covers that use.

  • Data accessed: read-only access to the owner's Gmail (gmail.readonly). The script searches for statement emails from banks and financial institutions and saves their attachments on the owner's computer.
  • Data collected: none. There are no servers, analytics, cookies, or third parties. The app runs entirely on the owner's own computer, and message content is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Sharing: nothing is shared, sold, or transferred to anyone.
  • Retention and deletion: the owner controls the downloaded files on his computer and can delete them, or revoke this app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, at any time.
  • Google API data: use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
